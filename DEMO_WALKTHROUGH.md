# Kyvo Collector Web — Demo Walkthrough

1. Open `http://localhost:5173/` and sign in as `agent@kyvo.test` with password `kyvo123`.
2. Review the Queue attention strip, lifecycle counts, search, filters, and priority sorting.
3. Open Amina Okafor to inspect the case drawer, balance, borrower context, timeline, and payment history.
4. Choose Contact borrower, preview Phone or WhatsApp, then save a `No Answer` outcome with a follow-up date.
5. Open the same case again and use the PTP contact outcome to record a promised amount and date.
6. Visit Follow-ups to review Today, Upcoming, Overdue, and Completed worklists.
7. Visit Notifications and mark updates read; confirm the navigation unread count changes.
8. Visit Performance to review period controls, recovery metrics, and the trend chart.
9. Sign out and sign in as `supervisor@kyvo.test` with password `kyvo123`.
10. Open Team to review collector workload. Use `/demo` directly to simulate partial/full payment, broken PTP, overdue follow-up, and reset the dataset.
